Discover a living heritage museum

Seven generations under one mud roof

Built in the early nineteenth century, the Gyapthago house was home to our family for nearly 200 years. Today, about 70% of the historic house has been turned into a heritage museum — preserving the memories, traditions and cultural identity of generations past.

  • The original smoke-darkened kitchen with its clay stove, copper pots and leather bellows
  • The underfloor granary and barley-flour store
  • Traditional dress, jewellery, coins, hats and old wooden furniture
  • The hand loom, spindles and wooden farm tools of village life
  • The prayer room and the rooftop where grain was dried in the sun

Authentic Ladakhi food — a tradition since 2006

Cooked the way our grandmothers cooked

Since 2006 the Gyapthago family has welcomed guests to taste the real flavours of Ladakh — pure, homemade, traditional Ladakhi cuisine, prepared with local barley, garden vegetables and the slow care of village hospitality.

  • SkyuButton-shaped pasta stewed with vegetables
  • ChhutagiBoat-shaped pasta in a root-vegetable broth
  • Barley SoupWarming soup of local barley
  • MomoSteamed dumplings, Ladakhi style
  • KhambirThick local brown bread
  • Gur-gur ChaSalted butter tea, churned by hand

Cook with the familyRoll skyu and chhutagi by hand, shape momos and churn butter tea in the gur-gur — join the family in the kitchen and learn how real Ladakhi food is made.

Local wine to tasteAsk the family for a glass of local Ladakhi wine with your meal.

Menu follows the season and what the family is cooking. Staying guests enjoy breakfast and dinner on our MAP plan, with lunch on request. Not staying with us? Visitors are welcome for lunch or dinner — just call or WhatsApp.

Explore the beauty of Stok

A day in Stok, with the family as your guide

Stay with us for a day and we'll take you through everything Stok has to offer — a peaceful village experience, without being far from Leh.

  1. Morning

    Breakfast & the 200-year-old home

    Ladakhi breakfast in the family kitchen, then a walk through the heritage museum — free for every guest.

  2. Late morning

    Stok Palace

    The historic palace of the Namgyal dynasty, with its royal heritage and museum.

  3. Midday

    Ladakhi lunch at home

    A traditional home-cooked meal back at Gyapthago — just ask and we’ll add it to your stay.

  4. Afternoon

    Stok Buddha Statue

    A magnificent Buddha with sweeping views over Leh and the surrounding mountains.

  5. Evening

    Village walks & trails

    Gentle permitted walks below Stok Kangri, past fields, chortens and traditional homes.

  6. Night

    Dinner & starry skies

    Ladakhi dinner by the family — included in your MAP stay — then the stillness of a mountain night.

The Stok Kangri summit trek has been closed for several years. Shorter walks are subject to local regulations and current access conditions.
